Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 8, 2026

Verso is a Bible reading and language-learning app for Spanish, English, Haitian Creole, Portuguese, Italian, German, and French. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

In short

Verso collects no personal data, and has no way to. The app does not request access to the internet, so there is nothing it could send anywhere, even if it tried. The only permission it asks you for is to show review-reminder notifications, and you can say no.

Every word you look up, every verse you read, stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Verso does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal data, device data, usage analytics, or advertising identifiers. The app does not include any advertising software development kit (SDK), analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service of any kind.

This isn't just a policy — it's a technical fact. Verso's Android app manifest does not declare the INTERNET permission, which means the operating system does not allow the app to open a network connection at all. There is no server Verso talks to, because it was never built to talk to one.

The permissions Verso does use are all local to your device:

  • Notifications — to remind you, at most once a day, when saved words are due for review. Android asks you first; you can decline, or turn reminders off any time in Verso's Settings.
  • Google Play billing — lets Verso hand an optional tip to the Google Play Store app on your phone, which takes the payment. Verso never sees your payment details.
  • Run at startup and prevent phone from sleeping — used only by Android's standard background-task scheduler so that the daily reminder check still happens after your phone restarts. Neither grants access to any of your data.

What's stored on your device

To function as a reading and study app, Verso saves a small amount of information locally on your device, using Android's standard private app-storage system. This information never leaves your device and is never seen by us:

  • Your chosen reading language and the language you're learning
  • Words you've bookmarked to your personal vocabulary list, and your practice progress on them
  • Your place in the Bible text, so the app can reopen where you left off
  • App preferences — theme, font size, reading-aloud speed, and chosen Bible translations
  • Your daily reading streak, and the date its latest reward (loaf or manna) was celebrated
  • Whether review reminders are on, and the date the last one was shown

This data is sandboxed to Verso by Android itself; other apps cannot read it, and none of it is ever sent to us — we don't operate any server.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup (for example, backup to your Google account), Android may include Verso's settings and saved words in that backup, so they can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. That backup is made and kept by Android under your account; Verso never sees it, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. Otherwise, uninstalling Verso deletes this information permanently.

Data sharing & third parties

Verso does not share data with anyone, for a simple reason: it does not collect any data to share. Apart from handing optional tips to Google Play (below), the app has no third-party integrations, no advertising network, no analytics provider, and no cloud backend.

Optional tips. Everything in Verso is free. Settings > Support Verso offers optional tips, which unlock nothing. If you choose to tip, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's own terms and privacy policy, using the payment method in your Google account. Verso receives no card, account or contact details and keeps no record of tips. To do this, Verso includes Google's Play Billing library; because Verso has no internet permission, neither Verso nor that library can send anything over the network — the Google Play Store app on your phone handles the purchase.

Verso's Bible texts, dictionaries, and word-study data are drawn from public domain and openly-licensed sources (such as public-domain and Creative Commons Bible translations, Wiktionary, PanLex, and Strong's Concordance). Using this content does not involve sending any of your data anywhere — it is bundled inside the app itself.

To pronounce words, Verso hands the word to the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (such as Google's or your phone maker's). That engine is part of Android, not of Verso, and works under its own provider's privacy terms; Verso itself sends nothing over the network.

Children's privacy

Verso does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age, including children. Because the app has no data collection mechanism at all, this applies uniformly to every user regardless of age.

Data security

Since no information is transmitted off your device, there is no data-in-transit to secure and no server-side storage to protect. The information described above is kept using the same operating-system-level protections Android applies to every app's private storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of Verso adds a feature that does involve network access — it will be updated on this same page, with a revised "last updated" date above. We will not retroactively change what this policy says about a past version of the app.
